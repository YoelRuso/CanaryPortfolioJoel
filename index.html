About me

I study on an island, so I drew this site as a chart of it.

I’m in the final year of Computer Engineering at ULPGC, the university of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria. I build educational animations and open-source tools that explain technical ideas visually.

I also enjoy full-stack and cloud projects, and I like designing and testing cloud architectures on my own machine.

As a kid I took apart every gadget I could, just to look at the circuit boards inside. I had no idea how they worked, but I loved them. I didn’t write a line of code until my first year at university, starting from zero. In my third year, a lecturer who taught software engineering with real passion got me hooked.

Now I build things that make my life easier and help me get better. After my degree, I want to start a company with my brother, building software and solutions for other businesses and people.

Projects

Each project is a buoy off the south coast. Open one and the chart turns to it.

TellMotion

An open-source skill for AI coding agents that turns an idea into a polished 2D explainer animation.

Project notes

You describe what to explain, pick a look from a theme catalog, and approve a storyboard before anything is animated. Each theme is a single JSON file that drives both the preview and the animation, so changing style is one command.

Built with Motion Canvas. Open source under the MIT license.
